# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Project: Voidlink (indie cyberpunk video game)**

**This Agreement is made on:** _________________________ (date)

**Between:**

**(1)** **Richard Martin**, sole trader, of [address to be supplied], United Kingdom ("the Disclosing Party"); and

**(2)** **___________________________________** of __________________________________________ ("the Receiving Party").

The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Purpose

The Disclosing Party intends to share confidential scripts, character notes, plot details, recording briefs, and other materials relating to the unreleased video game *Voidlink* with the Receiving Party for the purpose of evaluating, auditioning for, or performing voice acting work for the project (the **"Purpose"**). This Agreement governs the use of any such Confidential Information.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means any information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or in any other form, and whether or not marked confidential, including without limitation:

- character names, descriptions, backstories, story arcs, plot points, dialogue, and lore
- recording scripts, briefs, direction notes
- gameplay details, mechanics, release dates, and marketing plans
- the existence and content of this Agreement itself

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information which the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

a. was already in the Receiving Party's lawful possession before disclosure under this Agreement;
b. is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
c. was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to Confidential Information; or
d. was lawfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure.

## 4. Obligations

The Receiving Party shall:

a. keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential;
b. use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
c. not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party except to professional advisers (such as agents or accountants) bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations and on a strict need-to-know basis;
d. not post, publish, stream, broadcast, or otherwise share any Confidential Information on social media, podcasts, video content, or other public channels;
e. not record, photograph, or copy any Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose; and
f. apply at least the same standard of care to the Confidential Information as the Receiving Party applies to their own confidential information of similar importance (and in any event no less than a reasonable standard of care).

## 5. Return or destruction

On the earlier of (i) the Disclosing Party's written request or (ii) completion of the Purpose, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or, at the Disclosing Party's election, securely destroy all Confidential Information in their possession or control, including all copies, and confirm in writing that they have done so. The Receiving Party may retain one (1) copy of voice recordings they have made for the Purpose for their own showreel use, **provided** that any such use shall be deferred until **six (6) months after the public release of *Voidlink***, after which the recordings may be used in private showreel contexts only (not posted to public channels) without further consent.

## 6. Term

The obligations of confidentiality in this Agreement shall continue for **three (3) years** from the date of disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information, regardless of any earlier termination of the engagement between the parties.

## 7. No licence

Nothing in this Agreement transfers, licenses, or grants any rights in any Confidential Information or any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party. Any such rights remain at all times the property of the Disclosing Party.

## 8. No obligation to proceed

Neither party is obliged by this Agreement to enter into any further commercial or contractual arrangement. Casting, hiring, and performance terms (if any) shall be addressed in a separate agreement.

## 9. Remedies

The Receiving Party acknowledges that monetary damages may be inadequate to compensate the Disclosing Party for breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party may seek equitable relief, including injunction and specific performance, in addition to any other remedy available at law.

## 10. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **England and Wales**. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales for any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

## 11. Entire agreement

This Agreement sets out the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes any prior agreement or understanding, whether written or oral.

## 12. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is found by any court of competent jurisdiction to be unenforceable, that provision shall be severed and the remainder of the Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.
