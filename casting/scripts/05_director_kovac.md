# DIRECTOR KOVAC — Recording Script

**Project:** Voidlink (indie video game)
**Role:** Director of the Joint Cybersecurity Bureau (JCB) — international law enforcement against operatives
**Date drafted:** 2026-06-08
**Status:** Draft 1. Minor copy adjustments expected during direction.

## At a glance

- **Total word count:** ~200
- **Estimated total recorded audio:** ~90 seconds
- **Number of distinct cues:** 3 (one per ending family she appears in: ERASURE, CONTAINMENT, plus one shared opener)
- **Recommended session length:** 45 minutes inc. direction + pickups
- **Delivery format:** 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry, one file per cue, named `KOVAC_<cue_id>.wav`

## Voice direction

The institution speaking through one person. Senior, polite, professionally curious about what the player has become. **Not a villain. Not an ally. The institution.**

The character has read the player's file before this conversation. She knows every contract they have taken, every faction they have flirted with, every operative they have crossed. She is not surprised by anything they are. She is genuinely interested in *why*.

RP British works particularly well — institutional authority is the *point* for this role, and RP delivers that register reliably. American educated East Coast, Indian-English, or other authoritative-but-not-cold accents also fit. Authentic to the actor.

## Section B — Full recording script

### B.1 ERASURE Ending — Director Kovac welcomes the player to the JCB

The player has destroyed REVELATION and accepted the JCB's offer of a new identity. This is Kovac welcoming them, in a private meeting, at a generic-bureaucratic office.

**Cue K-001 — ERASURE welcome:**

*(brief opener — Kovac greeting someone she has been waiting to meet)*
> *"Please. Sit. I will not keep you long."*

*(measured — slightly amused at her own formality)*
> *"I have read your file. All of it. I would like, if I may, to ask you some questions that are not in it. You should know that the answers you give now go nowhere — not to a court, not to a record, not to your handler. They go only to me."*

*(beat — quietly genuine)*
> *"We are alone. Take your time."*

*(closing — the institutional warmth of someone who has done this many times)*
> *"The new identity is real. The bank account is real. The small apartment in a quiet city is real. The work, from here on, is also real — though you will not call it work. You will call it whatever you call it. We will not ask. Welcome."*

### B.2 CONTAINMENT Ending — Director Kovac signs off the Auditor

The player has accepted the Containment role — locking down REVELATION on Arunmor's behalf with full oversight authority. Kovac is signing off the appointment.

**Cue K-010 — CONTAINMENT confirmation:**

*(measured, professional)*
> *"Your appointment as principal independent auditor of the REVELATION programme is confirmed by this office and by the international cooperation framework that authorises it. You report to me and to two other people whose names I will give you in person."*

*(beat — slightly drier)*
> *"You will find your job easier than you expect, in two senses, and harder than you expect, in one. Mei Lin's people will cooperate with you in everything that does not threaten their work. That is the easy part. The hard part is the part where you have to decide what threatens the work and what doesn't. We will not tell you. That is the appointment."*

*(closing — the small concession of respect)*
> *"You are not the person I would have chosen. You are the person who got it. That is a different thing. I trust the second more than I would have trusted the first. Take care."*

### B.3 Optional — Brief Pre-Conversation Line

For any cinematic / overlay moment where Kovac needs a single line, this is a flexible neutral option.

**Cue K-020:**
> *"You should know that what you are about to hear from me is on the record only insofar as you choose it to be. I am not recording. Neither, I hope, are you."*

## Notes for the actor

- Kovac is *senior*. The reading register is institutional — long sentences, careful clause structure, a willingness to take a beat before a sensitive phrase. Avoid clipping the ends of sentences.
- She is genuinely curious about the player. Not paternalistic. Not condescending. The character has met many operatives and finds the player interesting enough to spend her time on.
- The phrase *"We are alone"* (Cue K-001) is a load-bearing line — it is the moment the player understands that whatever they say next does not have institutional consequence. Read it quietly. Not a threat. An offer.
- The Mei Lin reference (Cue K-010) is to the lead Arunmor research scientist. If Mei Lin is also being voiced, both actors should know the other is in the cast.
- *REVELATION* is read flat. No theatrical capitalisation in the voice. The institution does not speak in italics.
