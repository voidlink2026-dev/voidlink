# CIPHER — Recording Script

**Project:** Voidlink (indie video game)
**Role:** CIPHER — senior Underground operative, mentor to the player
**Date drafted:** 2026-06-08
**Status:** Draft 1. Minor copy adjustments expected during direction.

## At a glance

- **Total word count:** ~1,000
- **Estimated total recorded audio:** ~6 minutes
- **Number of distinct cues:** 24 (multiple bucket variants per scripted moment)
- **Recommended session length:** 90 minutes inc. direction + pickups
- **Delivery format:** 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry (no FX), one file per cue, named `CIPHER_<cue_id>.wav`

## Voice direction

Measured, weary, dryly intelligent. Senior tradesperson explaining the craft to an apprentice he half-expects not to make it. NOT macho, NOT hard-boiled. Restrained. Occasionally amused. Reads like real adult speech.

**Tone shifts to expect across the session:**
- Cues 1-3 → warm professional (Cipher writing to a *promising* operative)
- Cues 9-14 → neutral / measured (default state)
- Cues 15-17 → cooler, terse (Cipher disappointed but not angry)
- Cues 18-22 → the load-bearing letter (Arc 8 callback — Cipher confesses he knew)

### B.6 Arc 8 Lighthouse Callback — THE BIG ONE

Arc 8 reveals that Cipher has known about Voidlink Dispatch's profiling operation for nine years, and that his name was protected from the lighthouse index in 2189. This letter is his confession. Five pattern buckets; ALL FIVE should be recorded.

**Cue C-040 — strong principled bucket:**
> Subject: *the conversation I owed you*
>
> *"I told you, after the buyer list, that I owed you a conversation. I am paying that debt now, in writing, because writing is what I am good at and conversations are not. I knew about the lighthouse. I have known for nine years. The November 2189 protection note in the buyer index is, in a sense it is too late to apologise for, mine. I asked an old friend at Dispatch to keep my handle out of the inventory. I did not do the same for anyone else. I will not pretend I have a clean reason for that. What you have done with what you found is, in my opinion, the thing I should have done myself eight years ago. That you did it without being asked is, I think, the part that matters. I will not write you again unless you want me to. I am here if you want me to."* — C.

**Cue C-041 — weak principled bucket:**

**Cue C-042 — neutral bucket:**

**Cue C-043 — weak mercenary bucket:**

## Notes for the actor

- All cues are letters being typed and read. The player encounters them in their inbox as written text — your voice is what plays underneath, an *internal* reading by the player or an audio companion to the letter on screen. Direction is therefore **reading-aloud register**, not theatrical.
- The character occasionally signs as "C." (one initial) for cues to operatives he trusts, "Cipher" for the rest. The shift is meaningful — read C. with slightly more warmth than Cipher.
- The letters use em-dashes deliberately. Treat them as slight pauses, not as breaks.
- The phrase *"watch yourself"* appears multiple times. It is not a threat. It is the closest thing to affection Cipher allows himself.
- Italicised words in the script above are *not* for emphasis; they are for typographic clarity in this document. Direction in the session will indicate any true emphasis.

## Pickup window

One round of free pickups within 7 days of session for line errors, mispronunciations, or minor copy edits requested by the director.
