# YAAKOV STERN — Recording Script

**Project:** Voidlink (indie video game)
**Role:** YAAKOV STERN — in-fiction signatory of the Bond contract the player signs at intake
**Date drafted:** 2026-06-08
**Status:** Draft 1. Minor copy adjustments expected during direction.

## At a glance

- **Total word count:** ~250
- **Estimated total recorded audio:** ~2 minutes
- **Number of distinct cues:** 3 (Bond reading + optional opener + optional trailer line)
- **Recommended session length:** 45 minutes inc. direction + pickups
- **Delivery format:** 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry, one file per cue, named `STERN_<cue_id>.wav`

## Voice direction

The *gravitas voice* of the entire project. The first significant voiced moment the player hears in the game is Stern reading the Bond — the four rules the player is about to sign their identity to, irrevocably. **This is the most important single voiced moment in Voidlink.**

Reads the four rules deliberately, with weight, the way a notary reads a clause that the person signing does not yet fully understand. Not theatrical. Not portentous. Patient. Slightly sad, the way someone is when they know what the document means and the signer does not yet.

Ashkenazi / Eastern European Jewish heritage informs the name. Authentic accent — Russian / Polish / Czech / Hungarian / Israeli-tinged English all fit. British or American actors with this heritage absolutely qualify.

## Section B — Full recording script

### B.1 The Bond Reading — THE MOMENT

The player has just filled in their handle and email. They click the Bond viewer. Stern's voice reads the four rules over the typewriter on screen. The player can read silently in their head; Stern's reading is the *audible* version.

This is one continuous take, ~90 seconds. The cue is broken into beats here for direction; in delivery it should feel like a single measured reading.

**Cue S-001 — the Bond, in full:**

*(measured opening — addressed to whoever is reading)*
> *"To the operatives of the Voidlink International contractor network. By signature you agree to the following, irrevocably, anonymously, and without right of resignation."*

*(beat — Rule One)*
> *"One. Voidlink International is entitled to its contracted percentage of every transaction effected through the network. This percentage shall be twelve percent."*

*(beat — Rule Two — slightly heavier; this is the rule about arbitration and the prohibition on outside enforcement)*
> *"Two. Disputes between operatives, between operatives and clients, or between operatives and Voidlink International shall be resolved through Voidlink arbitration. Outside enforcement is itself a breach of this Bond. The arbitrator's decision is final."*

*(beat — Rule Three — this rule is morally contested; deliver flat, not approvingly)*
> *"Three. Operatives may take contracts from any client. Discrimination based on client identity, alignment, or stated purpose is prohibited. The operative is free to decline. The operative is not free to refuse on the basis of who is asking."*

*(beat — Rule Four — the heaviest; pace down)*
> *"Four. Killing other operatives outside the sanctioned arbitration process is grounds for the immediate, permanent, and public revocation of operative status. The revocation may include physical sanctions. No appeal will be heard. No statute of limitations applies."*

*(closing — read as a signature line; the slight weariness here is the entire point of the character)*
> *"Signed by hardware identity hash. Recorded irrevocably. We are not changing this document."*

*(quietly, the last line)*
> *"Yaakov Stern. February twenty-one eighty-three."*

## Notes for the actor

- The Bond reading is **the load-bearing voiced moment**. Patience is everything. Take your time. Pauses between rules are deliberate.
- The character's name (Yaakov Stern) is pronounced *Yah-kov Shtern* (the surname Anglicises easily to *Stern* — your call which to use; we recommend the un-anglicised "Shtern" as it lands the character's heritage harder, but English-speaking Stern is also acceptable).
- The year *2183* is read as *twenty-one eighty-three*, not *two thousand one hundred eighty-three*.
- The slight weariness on *"We are not changing this document"* is intentional. The character has seen the rules tested and held; the line carries the weight of every time it has needed to hold.
- This recording will be the first significant voiced moment a new player ever hears. Approach it that way.
