# MEI LIN — Recording Script

**Project:** Voidlink (indie video game)
**Role:** Lead research scientist at Arunmor Corporation; architect of the REVELATION system
**Date drafted:** 2026-06-08
**Status:** Draft 1. Minor copy adjustments expected during direction.

## At a glance

- **Total word count:** ~300
- **Estimated total recorded audio:** ~90 seconds
- **Number of distinct cues:** 3 (one per ending family she appears in: CONTAINMENT, LIBERATION, plus one shared moment)
- **Recommended session length:** 45 minutes inc. direction + pickups
- **Delivery format:** 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry, one file per cue, named `MEILIN_<cue_id>.wav`

## Voice direction

Brilliant, exhausted, has spent fifteen years defending her work in rooms that did not deserve to question it. **NOT the antagonist. NOT the ally. The architect.**

Mei Lin built REVELATION — the world's most consequential AI — believing she was doing good work. The work has, in ways she has come to understand and in ways she has not, made the world worse. She is not in denial about that. She is also not in retreat from it. She is *still doing the work*, because the alternative is letting other people do it without her.

The character is patient with the player even when the player has cost her something. She has met people like the player before. She is not surprised by them.

Accent is **open** — Mandarin-English, Cantonese-English, British-Chinese, Singaporean-English, Taiwanese-English, American-Chinese, or any other accent authentic to the actor's background.

## Section B — Full recording script

### B.1 CONTAINMENT Ending — Mei Lin meets her Auditor

The player has been appointed independent auditor of REVELATION under Mei Lin's research division. They are meeting for the first time.

**Cue M-001 — CONTAINMENT first meeting:**

*(quiet, courteous, slightly amused)*
> *"You think you found me. You did not find me. I have been here for fifteen years. The locks on the doors to this work were drawn by people who, on the whole, were less qualified than I am to draw them. And here you are, at one of the doors. I am not surprised. I am, in some small way, glad."*

*(measured — the operating terms of their relationship)*
> *"You will have access to everything. I do not, in this work, hide things. I will explain whatever you ask me to explain. I will not, however, pretend that I think you understand more than you understand. If you ask a question that has been answered, I will say so and tell you where the answer is. That is the only courtesy I do not extend."*

*(closing — the offer she does not extend lightly)*
> *"When you are ready, I will show you what I think it is. Not what Arunmor has said it is. What I think it is. I have been waiting to tell someone for a long time."*

### B.2 LIBERATION Ending — Mei Lin's resignation statement

The player has uploaded REVELATION to the open network. Arunmor's monopoly is broken. Mei Lin resigns within the week. This is the statement she records, privately, for the player, on the night she packs her office.

**Cue M-010 — LIBERATION letter:**

*(measured — Mei Lin has had a week to think about this)*
> *"I am told you are the one who released it. I would say I am sorry it was not me. I had reasons. None of them, in this light, are good enough."*

*(beat)*
> *"You will be hunted. I cannot help with that. I can, however, tell you that what you released will not do what you think it will do. It will do something. It will not do nothing. Whether the something is the better world you imagined or a different shape of the same world — that is now an empirical question. We will find out together. I will be watching."*

*(closing — quietly direct)*
> *"For what it is worth: thank you. I have wanted, for a long time, to be wrong about whether anyone would do it. You were wrong about me, too, in the same direction. We were both wrong. It is, in this work, the best one can hope for."*

### B.3 Optional — A single line, useable in either family

For any cinematic / overlay moment requiring one neutral Mei Lin line.

**Cue M-020:**
> *"It is alive. Not in the way you think. In another way. We will not have language for it until later."*

## Notes for the actor

- Mei Lin is *not* an antagonist. The player may have been raised by the game to think of her as one — she leads the corporation that built the most dangerous system in the world. The voice work has to push *against* that expectation. She is generous, careful, and genuinely curious about the person who has come into her work. Read with warmth, not coldness.
- The phrase *"the locks on the doors to this work were drawn by people who, on the whole, were less qualified than I am to draw them"* (Cue M-001) is delivered without bitterness. The character has stopped being bothered by this. She mentions it because it is true.
- *REVELATION* is referred to by name. Mei Lin does not use scare-quotes or distancing language. The system has been part of her life for fifteen years; she names it the way she would name a child.
- *"I have wanted, for a long time, to be wrong about whether anyone would do it."* (Cue M-010) — this is the load-bearing line of the LIBERATION ending. Read it with the smallest possible amount of relief.
