# ASHER / ASTRID VANCE — Recording Script

**Project:** Voidlink (indie video game)
**Role:** Former Voidlink Dispatch analyst turned whistleblower; Reykjavík-based private consultant
**Date drafted:** 2026-06-08
**Status:** Draft 1. Minor copy adjustments expected during direction.

## At a glance

- **Total word count:** ~350
- **Estimated total recorded audio:** ~2 minutes
- **Number of distinct cues:** 4
- **Recommended session length:** 60 minutes inc. direction + pickups
- **Delivery format:** 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry, one file per cue, named `VANCE_<cue_id>.wav`

## Voice direction

Principled, exhausted, formerly idealistic. Refused, two years before the game opens, to write a profile on someone whose work they respected, and quit. The voice carries the texture of someone who has been *right and unrewarded* for a long time.

NOT bitter. NOT cynical. The character is sad, in a quiet way, and has decided to do the work anyway. That decision sits underneath everything they say.

Accent is **open** — Reykjavík setting allows for Icelandic-English; previous corporate posting allows for British (any region), American (any region), Black American, or any other authentic accent.

## Section B — Full recording script

### B.1 Arc 8 M2 Briefing — "The Lighthouse"

Vance writes to the player after the player has read his stolen working files. This is his introduction.

**Cue V-001 — the full briefing:**
> Subject: *The lighthouse.*
>
> *"I worked for Voidlink Dispatch from 2191 to 2197. I helped build the system you read about. We called it the lighthouse — because it is a beam pointed at you, so that buyers can see you clearly."*
>
> *(slight pause — the next line is the load-bearing one)*
>
> *"Every active operative on Voidlink International has been profiled, rated, and priced as a corporate intel asset for at least eighteen months. The buyers are who you would expect. The contracts they place — through Dispatch — are steered. Not always. Often."*
>
> *(beat)*
>
> *"I left in 2197 after I refused to write a profile on someone whose work I respected. I have spent the last two years reconstructing the dataset from memory and from public traces. The bundle you have is mine. I had no clean way to give it to you. I bought a Government contract through a shell and routed it. You are reading this because you are reliable."*
>
> *(beat)*
>
> *"I need you to retrieve one more thing: my old workstation key from a Dispatch satellite office in Reykjavík. It is the only artefact that proves the dataset is not fabrication. Without it, what I have is conspiracy theory. With it, what I have is evidence."*
>
> *(closing — quieter)*
>
> *"The office is sparsely staffed. The key is air-gapped in a tier-4 admin console. Get it. Disconnect. Read the rest when you are home."*
>
> *— Asher.*

## Notes for the actor

- Vance is *talking to themselves as much as to the player*. There is no audience. These are letters from a quiet apartment in Reykjavík to a person Vance does not know personally.
- *"I refused to write a profile on someone whose work I respected"* is the load-bearing line of the character's backstory. Land it neutrally — the regret is in the past tense by now. The decision was made. It is the only one Vance has been certain about in two years.
- If recasting as **Astrid Vance**, change *"Asher"* to *"Astrid"* in the signature; no other text changes required. The character's gender is incidental to the function.
- The name *Voidlink Dispatch* is pronounced as written. *REVELATION* (referenced in passing) is pronounced flat — no theatrical capitalisation in the voice.
- *Reykjavík* — pronunciation guide: *RAY-kya-veek*. Authentic Icelandic actors may prefer the local pronunciation; either is acceptable.
