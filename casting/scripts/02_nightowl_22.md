# NIGHTOWL_22 — Recording Script

**Project:** Voidlink (indie video game)
**Role:** NIGHTOWL_22 — independent broker, Lagos-based, running an eleven-year deception
**Date drafted:** 2026-06-08
**Status:** Draft 1. Minor copy adjustments expected during direction.

## At a glance

- **Total word count:** ~600
- **Estimated total recorded audio:** ~4 minutes
- **Number of distinct cues:** 8
- **Recommended session length:** 60 minutes inc. direction + pickups
- **Delivery format:** 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry, one file per cue, named `NIGHTOWL_<cue_id>.wav`

## Voice direction

The character's canon line is *"her voice is older than her handle suggests."* That's the whole brief. She sounds worn-in, not glamorous; carries weight without performing authority; slightly amused at most things. Nigerian or UK-Nigerian diaspora — authentic accent only; NOT a performed "Hollywood African" accent.

Eleven years running a careful deception that has kept eighty-three thousand strangers alive — that backstory should sit underneath every read, even when she is being transactional.

### B.3 Arc 7 "Lunch" — THE LOAD-BEARING CALL

This is the most important NightOwl moment in the game. She telephones the player on an encrypted relay after they complete Arc 7 Mission 2. Voice-only call — no inbox letter, no typewriter. This should land as a *person actually speaking*, intimate, slightly amused at her own situation. Pauses matter.

**Cue N-020 — the call:**
> *(slight pause, breath)*
>
> *"I started LANTERN_BRIDGE eleven years ago. I was trying to keep a small company alive. I broker leaks to whichever side is winning, so the other side stays in the fight. The companies do not know they are being managed. Neither does the Mesh."*
>
> *(pause — beat for the player to ask why she is telling them)*
>
> *"Because Internic's CTO is forty-eight hours from realising the leaks are coming from one source. When he does, the war ends. One of the two companies wins it outright. They then move on to whoever is in their way next. The current ‘whoever' is a labour cooperative in the South China Sea, eighty-three thousand people, with a medical study Δ5 wants. The war has been keeping them alive."*
>
> *(longer pause)*
>
> *"You have the cache now. You can deliver it to Internic and let Δ5 collapse. You can deliver it to Δ5 and let Internic be sued into nothing. You can expose me, and the war ends however the more vicious of the two finishes it. Or you can help me keep the leaks going for one more cycle. Until I can move Helios somewhere safer."*
>
> *(beat)*
>
> *"No outcome here is clean. I am not asking you to pretend it is. I am asking you to choose with all of the facts. You have forty-eight hours. The next contract will record what you choose."*

## Notes for the actor

- The "Lunch" call (Cue N-020) is the most important NightOwl moment in the game. The player encounters everything else NightOwl says as written text in their inbox; the "Lunch" cue is **a phone call**. Treat it as actually speaking to a person. Be willing to take long pauses.
- She is *not* angry. She is *not* repentant. She has been running this operation for eleven years and has made peace with what it costs her. The texture is *practical exhaustion plus a faint amusement*.
- The names: NIGHTOWL_22 (read as "Night-Owl twenty-two"), LANTERN_BRIDGE (read as "Lantern-Bridge"). Pronounce as single compound words.
- *Helios Marine* is the labour cooperative she's protecting. Pronounce *hee-lee-oss*.
- *Δ5* (delta-five) refers to "Arunmor Subsidiary Five." She pronounces it "*delta five*."
